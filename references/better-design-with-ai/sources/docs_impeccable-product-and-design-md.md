## What it says
- **Two files, two jobs.** `init` "records durable product truth in `PRODUCT.md`"; visual systems "are recorded separately in `DESIGN.md`". Init "does not invent a visual world".
- **PRODUCT.md sections:** Platform, Stack, Users, Product Purpose, Positioning, Operating Context, Capabilities and Constraints, Brand Commitments, Evidence on Hand, Product Principles, Accessibility & Inclusion.
- **Interview limits:** "at most three focused questions" per round; label every inferred fact.
- **DESIGN.md** follows the Google Labs spec; `document` generates it "from existing project code", and when one exists, "do not silently overwrite it".
- **When to write DESIGN.md:** an incumbent system exists without one, a first build's decisions need recording, the design has drifted, or before a large redesign.
- **Operate surfaces** (the visitor came to get a job done): the visual "world lends four things and only these: type, palette, density, and one signature move. It never supplies the layout, the navigation model, or the controls."
- **Real content:** comps use "realistic data with plausible values and units, real labels, and one state worth seeing", "never placeholder tiles".

## For better design with AI
- Keep product facts (users, purpose, constraints) apart from visual tokens; agents mix them up otherwise.
- For internal tools, let style change only type, palette, density, and one signature move; keep standard controls.
