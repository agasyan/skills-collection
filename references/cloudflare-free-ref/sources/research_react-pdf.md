## What it says

- **Layout:** "Yoga brings flexbox to the page: rows, columns, wrapping and absolute positioning."
- **Features:** "Custom fonts, images, SVG, form fields, hyphenation and page-break control."

## For an internal system
- Use it when you need a real PDF file to email or store, not just a printout.
- Build the PDF in the browser, then upload it to R2 through the Worker as the issued copy.
