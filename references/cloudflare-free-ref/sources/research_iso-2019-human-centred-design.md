# ISO 9241-210:2019 Ergonomics of human-system interaction, Part 210: Human-centred design for interactive systems
ISO/TC 159/SC 4 (2019). International standard, 2nd edition, 33 pages. https://committee.iso.org/standard/77520.html (preview only: contents, foreword, introduction, scope)
Type: research · Read: 2026-10-06

## What it says
- Requirements and recommendations for human-centred design across the whole life cycle of interactive systems, written for the people who plan and manage design projects. Its scope names "custom office systems" and web applications. Read via the official preview: https://normy.normoff.gov.sk/norma/130333/nahlad
- Gives an overview of activities, not detailed methods. The 2019 edition is a minor revision of 2010 (clearer Figure 1, more on accessibility).

## Findings
- **Six principles.** Design rests on an explicit understanding of users, tasks and environments; users are involved throughout; user-centred evaluation drives and refines the design; the process is iterative; the design covers the whole user experience; the team brings multidisciplinary skills.
- **Four activities.** Understand and specify the context of use; specify user requirements; produce design solutions; evaluate the design.
- **Three ways to evaluate.** User-based testing, inspection-based evaluation, and long-term monitoring.
- **Works with any process.** The approach "can be incorporated in approaches as diverse as object-oriented, waterfall and rapid application development."
- **Stable core.** The principles and activities "have not changed substantially since ISO 13407" and "have been validated by ten years of application."
- **Claimed payoff.** Better effectiveness, efficiency, user satisfaction, accessibility and well-being. The introduction states this without data.

## For internal systems
- Before building, write down who uses the tool, for which tasks, and in what setting (desk, shared screen, peak hours).
- Turn that context into user requirements, then design against them.
- Evaluate every iteration with real staff and with an inspection pass, and keep monitoring usage after launch.
