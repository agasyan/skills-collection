## What it says
- **ExcelJS:** "Read, manipulate and write spreadsheet data and styles to XLSX and JSON": fonts, borders, fills, number formats, formulas. It publishes two browserified bundles in `dist/`.
- **ExcelJS activity:** no push since January 2025.
- **SheetJS Community Edition** "remains truly open source under the Apache 2.0 License". Install it from the SheetJS CDN: "the registry is out of date. The latest version on that registry is 0.18.5."
- **SheetJS styling is Pro:** "SheetJS Pro offers solutions beyond data processing: … let out your inner Picasso with styling."

## For an internal system
- Use ExcelJS for a styled download: bold headers, number formats, a totals row.
- Use SheetJS CE or plain CSV for data-only exports; basic-Excel staff open both in Excel.
