# Write Simply
Paul Graham (2021). paulgraham.com. https://www.paulgraham.com/simply.html
Type: blog · Read: 2026-10-06

## Core idea
Use ordinary words and simple sentences: the less energy readers spend on the prose, the more they have left for the ideas.
Evidence: none cited

## Points
- **Deeper engagement.** The easier something is to read, the more deeply readers engage with it.
- **Readers finish.** Most readers' energy flags partway through; low friction keeps more of them reading to the end.
- **Considerate.** Fancy writing makes readers do extra work so the writer can seem cool.
- **Non-native readers.** Many readers understand ideas better than they understand English, so a hard topic doesn't license hard words.
- **Keeps you honest.** Fancy writing can hide a lack of ideas; "If you say nothing simply, it will be obvious to everyone, including you."
- **Lasts longer.** Future readers, like foreign readers today, will face a changed language and culture.
- **Edit by cutting.** He drafts fast, then spends days editing, and much of that editing is cutting.
- **Complexity on purpose only.** Use a complicated sentence or fancy word for effect, never by accident.

## For writing docs
- Choose the ordinary word; a hard topic still gets easy words.
- Draft fast, then edit mostly by cutting.
- Write a complex sentence only when you chose it on purpose.
